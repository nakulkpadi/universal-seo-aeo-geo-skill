# Growth Intelligence
When explicit exports are supplied, combine GSC/analytics/rank/backlink/conversion data with the
audit. Support query-to-page mapping, cannibalization, decay, CTR opportunity, content gaps,
internal-link opportunities, backlink quality and business-value prioritization. Use:
opportunity = demand × attainable CTR/rank upside × business value × confidence ÷ effort.
Treat this as prioritization, never guaranteed traffic/revenue.
